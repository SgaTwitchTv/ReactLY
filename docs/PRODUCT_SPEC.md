# ReactLY product specification

## Product statement

ReactLY is the social platform where the reaction is the content.

## Core problem

Online reactions are emotionally engaging but awkward to create. Creators
currently screen-record, record themselves separately, and edit both sources in
a third-party tool. ReactLY turns that workflow into a native format.

## Core loop

1. Choose source content from ReactLY or elsewhere.
2. Record a genuine front-camera reaction.
3. ReactLY composes a recognizable vertical Rect.
4. Preview and publish or share it.
5. Other users can react to the resulting Rect, producing a reaction chain.

## Initial audience

- Gen Z and young adult social-media users
- Friend groups sharing jokes, purchases, scores, outfits, announcements, and
  hot takes
- Creators who already publish reaction content

## MVP

- Select a local photo or short video
- Record front camera and microphone
- Maintain the source-first creator view
- Render the canonical exported timeline
- Preview, discard, re-record, and save locally
- Operate without an account or backend

## Later

- Android React Anywhere screen capture
- iOS share-extension/import workflow
- Profiles and following
- Feed and reactions
- Publishing and moderation
- Reaction trees
- Participant approval workflow

## Explicitly out of Phase 1

- Authentication
- Cloud upload
- Social graph
- Likes and comments
- Direct messaging
- Monetization
- Covert recording behavior

## Product vocabulary

- **Rect**: the finished reaction video.
- **Source**: the photo, video, score, post, or screen content being reacted to.
- **Reaction**: front-camera video and microphone audio.
- **Source-first view**: source full-screen, camera in the bottom-right.
- **Reaction-first view**: reaction full-screen, source in the lower-left.
- **React Anywhere**: future cross-app capture mode.
